Call for the UN to Establish an "Office of Extraterrestrial Civil Affairs"

聯合國地球外文明事務部

來源: YouTube Shorts

Our Urgent Call / 我們的緊急呼籲

We urgently call on the United Nations to formally establish a dedicated agency to handle all matters between humanity and potentially existing extraterrestrial civilizations. We propose the following names:

Office of Extraterrestrial Civil Affairs
或
UN Cosmic Civil Affairs Liaison Office

我們緊急呼籲聯合國正式成立一個專責機構，處理人類與可能存在的外星文明之間的一切事務。我們建議名稱為：

「地球外文明事務部」(Office of Extraterrestrial Civil Affairs)
或
「聯合國宇宙文明聯絡署」(UN Cosmic Civil Affairs Liaison Office)

The Agency's Mission / 這個機構的使命

  • Centralize global UAP data — Build a transparent, public database
    統整全球 UAP 公開資料——建立透明、公開的資料庫
  • Establish a cross-border research platform — Enable scientists worldwide to participate, not just military agencies
    建立跨國研究平台——讓各國科學家共同參與、非軍事壟斷
  • Promote peaceful space research — Science-based, peace-oriented
    推動和平性宇宙研究——以科研為本、以和平為目標
  • Develop common principles for humanity's first contact with extraterrestrial civilizations — Prevent unilateral competition that could trigger conflict
    制定人類首次接觸外星文明的共同原則——避免各國私下競逐引發衝突
  • Protect the public's right to know — Transparency prevents the fear that comes from secrecy
    保護公眾知的權利——資訊透明，避免恐慌的根源是不透明

Why Now? / 為什麼現在？

Throughout history, every major turning point for humanity — from the Age of Exploration to the Internet Age — required new frameworks for international cooperation. Today, as we may be watched by extraterrestrial civilizations across the cosmos, we need unity more than ever.

歷史上，人類每一次重大轉折——從航海時代到網路時代——都需要新的國際合作框架。今天，面對可能被外星文明注視的宇宙，我們比以往任何時候都更需要團結。

True fear comes from the unknown, not from truth itself.
真正的恐懼來自未知，而非真相本身。

Establishing an internationally supervised, science-driven, peace-first agency will not cause chaos — it will make the world more stable. Because when we cooperate for the first time as "Earthlings," it will mark the true maturity of human civilization.

建立一個受到國際監督、科學导向、和平優先的機構，不僅不會造成混亂，反而能讓世界更穩定。因為當我們第一次以「地球人」的身份合作，這將是人類文明真正的成熟。

Our Demand / 我們的訴求

Please sign this petition so the United Nations can hear our voice.

請簽署這份連署書，讓聯合國聽見我們的聲音。

We want our descendants to be represented to extraterrestrial civilizations not by weapons and military power, but by wisdom, peace, and civilizational maturity.

我們希望我們的後代在面對外星文明時，代表地球的不是武器與軍事力量，而是智慧、和平與文明成熟度。

Because / 因為

Because a truly advanced civilization's most important ability is not to conquer the universe — but to learn to stop destroying each other.

因為一個真正進步的文明，最重要的能力不是征服宇宙，而是學會不再互相毀滅。
